# Security and research governance

## Data handling

Research projects are private on creation. Collect only necessary personal information. Keep participant identifiers out of general reports and de-identify research datasets before upload. Consent forms, clinical records and appointment evidence need separate restricted storage and retention rules.

Public visibility requires deliberate release of selected artifacts. Researcher ownership does not establish permission to publish third-party material or participant data.

## Institutional review

Record academic proposal review, ethics clearance, site permission and final report review separately. SavePles stores evidence and review decisions; authority remains with the relevant institution or external body.

The PNG Health Research Portal directs health research to the Medical Research Advisory Committee for review. PNGIMR has its own Institutional Review Board process for its research. Do not assume PNGIMR is the reviewing body for every student project.

For a DWU pilot, confirm the current institutional process, designated reviewers and requirements directly with DWU before enabling institutional approval labels. Requirements vary by study and institution.

## Access and application security

- Verify identity and authorization on every protected operation.
- Restrict role appointment and privilege changes; audit both.
- Enforce organization and project boundaries in queries and file access.
- Validate uploads by size, type and contents; quarantine files pending checks.
- Rate-limit authentication, uploads, search and assistant requests.
- Protect sessions and state-changing requests; keep secrets outside source control.
- Treat document instructions as untrusted data when used by the assistant.
- Prevent public caching of private responses and downloads.
- Log resource identifiers and outcomes without copying research contents.

## Archive governance

Show authorship, affiliation, release date, version and review status. Institution-approved does not mean externally peer-reviewed. Provide reporting, correction, rights disputes and withdrawal procedures. Preserve attribution when revising records.

Choose access terms and licenses per release. No project-wide open-source or content license is selected in this initial documentation.

## Operational readiness

Before handling real data, agree institutional ownership, consent wording, retention periods, account closure, incident response contacts, backup policy, restoration checks and any geographic hosting restrictions. Document who can access support cases and how exceptional access is authorized.

Use synthetic or explicitly authorized sample data for demonstrations. Never commit research datasets, personal records, credentials or clearance evidence to the public source repository.
