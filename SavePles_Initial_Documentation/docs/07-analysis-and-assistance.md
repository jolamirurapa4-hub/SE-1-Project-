# Analysis and research assistance

## Statistical workflow

1. Upload an authorized, de-identified dataset.
2. Preview rows, variable types, coding rules and missing values.
3. Record cleaning operations without overwriting the original.
4. Select the research question, variables and analysis method.
5. Check assumptions and show warnings or reasons the method cannot run.
6. Run a versioned calculation and retain its provenance.
7. Review results with an appropriately qualified person.
8. Export tables, charts, methods, limitations and provenance.

Begin with descriptive statistics and charts. Add correlations and inferential methods only with documented input requirements, reference datasets and numerical checks.

Report sample size, exclusions, missing-data handling and uncertainty where applicable. Do not equate correlation with causation. A small p-value alone does not establish practical importance or research quality. Avoid repeated test selection to obtain a preferred result.

## Assistant responsibilities

The assistant may draft templates, explain methods, suggest data checks and help researchers identify limitations. Numerical claims must be grounded in the recorded analysis outputs. Research text and changes remain subject to researcher review.

It cannot approve ethics applications, verify research truth, invent citations, fabricate observations, guarantee valid findings or modify raw data silently. Record material assistance in the project's provenance in accordance with institutional policy.

## Learning the research process

Implement project memory as researcher-approved records of methods, variable definitions and preferences. Users can inspect, correct and delete those records. Memory is scoped to the project and its current access permissions.

Do not train a shared model on private submissions by default. Any future reuse requires an explicit, separately governed consent process.

## Consultation guidance

Use a maintained directory of official sources, applicability rules and last verification dates. The assistant explains why an organization may be relevant and routes uncertain cases to a supervisor. It must not claim approval, invent contacts or submit applications without explicit authorization.

## Evaluation before release

Validate calculations against trusted reference outputs, test missing and malformed data, and assess assistant recommendations with faculty reviewers. Check invented references, unsupported conclusions, permission leakage and misleading claims of approval. Uploaded documents are untrusted content and cannot override access rules or tool permissions.
