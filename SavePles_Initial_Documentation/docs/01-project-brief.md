# Project brief

Status: initial specification | Date: 12 November 2026

## Purpose

Provide students and faculty in Papua New Guinea with an accessible place to conduct, review and archive research. Preserve student work that may otherwise remain in personal storage or isolated departmental collections.

## Problem to validate

Existing journals, institutional publication pages and specialist research portals serve important purposes. Student researchers may still lack a common workspace for drafts, data analysis, feedback and controlled sharing across institutions. Interviews must establish the extent of these barriers before SavePles claims national coverage or institutional adoption.

## Objectives

1. Preserve research outputs with authorship, affiliations, versions and access controls.
2. Support structured research from proposal through final report.
3. Make collaboration and institutional review traceable.
4. Provide reproducible calculations and clear explanations of statistical results.
5. Improve discovery of work researchers are authorized to share.
6. Direct researchers to relevant institutional and PNG review bodies using verified references.

## Users

Students, co-researchers, supervisors, Heads of Department, institutional reviewers, archive moderators and readers. Institutional partnerships and reviewer appointments are not yet confirmed.

## Pilot case

A DWU physiotherapy research team creates a private project and records its research question, methodology, proposed analysis and consent arrangements. A student coordinator manages contributions. A verified supervisor or HoD reviews the proposal. Applicable ethics and site permissions are recorded before relevant data collection begins.

The team imports a de-identified dataset and reviews the resulting analysis with its supervisor. A final report version is submitted for review. After approval and confirmation of sharing rights, the team may request publication of its report. Raw participant data remains restricted unless separately authorized for release.

This is a proposed pilot scenario, not an announced DWU partnership.

## Scope

The initial release covers accounts, organizations, research workspaces, versioned uploads, review records and controlled archiving. Statistical tooling and assistant features follow in later milestones.

Excluded from the initial release: journal accreditation, automatic ethics approval, clinical decision support, guaranteed research validity, native mobile apps and model training on submitted research.

## Success measures

During the pilot, record project completion, review turnaround, successful retrieval of archived work, user-reported barriers and accessibility on commonly used phones and networks. Set numeric adoption targets after the pilot population and capacity are agreed.
