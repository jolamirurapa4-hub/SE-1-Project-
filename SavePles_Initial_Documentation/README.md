# SavePles

**A community research workspace and archive for Papua New Guinea.**

SavePles helps students develop research projects, collaborate with peers, analyse data, obtain institutional review and preserve their work. Researchers control who can see their projects and which approved outputs they share publicly.

## Project status

Initial planning and specifications, dated 12 November 2026. This repository contains documentation and the approved project emblem. Application code, infrastructure and live services have not been implemented.

## Why SavePles

Research outputs are available through several PNG institutions, journals and specialist portals. SavePles addresses a proposed gap between doing student research and making that work discoverable: a shared workspace that supports the research process and preserves outputs across disciplines.

The scale of this gap, publication delays and barriers to student participation still require validation with students, faculty and institutional libraries.

## Planned capabilities

- Private research projects with structured proposals, methods, results and references.
- Organizations for institutions, departments, year groups and research teams.
- Versioned documents, datasets and review submissions.
- Recorded proposal review, required clearance evidence and final report review.
- CSV/Excel import, reproducible statistical analysis and charts.
- AI assistance for templates, method explanations and validation suggestions.
- A searchable public archive with clear authorship and review labels.

## Working principles

Projects begin privately. Review decisions apply to an exact submitted version. Approval does not automatically publish a paper. Research approval, ethics clearance and peer review are distinct records. Research data is never committed to this public repository.

Technology choices are proposed and will be confirmed during implementation. See the delivery plan for decisions that remain open.
